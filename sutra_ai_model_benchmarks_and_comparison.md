## 🔍 Detailed Provider Profiles

### 1. OpenAI Engine Suite
* **GPT 6 Astra (gpt-6-luna)**: Default flagship model for general interview dominance, combining high speed (~210ms) with state-of-the-art technical reasoning.
* **GPT 5.6 Sol (gpt-5.6)**: Highly structured responses for distributed systems, database internals, and architecture reviews.
* **OpenAI o3-mini (gptoss)**: Step-by-step mathematical, dynamic programming, and algorithmic problem-solving.

### 2. Google Gemini Suite
* **Gemini 3.8 Flash (gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts)**: Tailored specifically for sub-220ms conversational turnaround during live audio interviews.
* **Gemini 3.7 Flash (gemini-3.7-flash)**: High-accuracy hybrid reasoning for both technical and behavioral interview scenarios.

### 3. Anthropic Claude Suite
* **Claude Haiku 4.5 (claude-haiku)**: Instant spoken responses with concise delivery and zero AI preamble.
* **Claude Sonnet 5 (claude-sonnet)**: Deep coding, code explanation, edge cases, and algorithmic verification.

### 4. Meta / Groq LPU Suite
* **Llama 4 Scout (llama-4-scout)**: Blazing fast inference powered by Groq LPUs.
* **GPT-OSS 20B (gpt-oss-20b)**: Sub-130ms turnaround for immediate coding snippets and quick hints.
